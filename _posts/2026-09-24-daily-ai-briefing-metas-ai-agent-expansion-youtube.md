## Meta Goes All-In on AI Agent Muse

Meta is making a huge push for its AI agent, Muse, aiming to integrate it deeply into our daily lives. CEO Mark Zuckerberg announced that Muse will be a core feature of Meta's AI glasses. They're also rolling out new, lighter, camera-free versions of these glasses with an impressive 12-hour battery life, which sounds perfect for all-day use without feeling weighed down. And for those who might want a dedicated AI companion, Meta even unveiled a Tamagotchi-like wearable device, giving Muse yet another mobile home. This strategy is all about making AI more accessible and ubiquitous, whether it's through a smart wearable or glasses.

## YouTube's AI Revolution for Viewers and Creators

YouTube is getting a major AI upgrade across the board, making both watching and creating content much smarter. For music lovers, YouTube Music is introducing "Ask Music," allowing you to describe what you want to hear in plain English, no more struggling with exact song titles or artists. Beyond music, YouTube is letting you "build your own algorithm" with AI. You'll be able to describe the type of videos you want to see, and Gemini will craft a personalized feed just for you – talk about taking control of your recommendations! And for the creators out there, new AI features in YouTube Studio will help generate video ideas and even monitor thumbnail performance, giving you powerful tools to grow your channel.

## ChatGPT Mobile App Gets Agentic Power

Your ChatGPT mobile app is about to get a lot more powerful, especially for Pro and Plus users. OpenAI is rolling out new voice-based "agentic features" to the Work tab on your phone. This means you'll be able to tell ChatGPT to complete more complex, multi-step tasks directly from your mobile device, using just your voice. Think of it as having a super-smart assistant in your pocket that can actually *do* things for you, not just answer questions. This could be a game-changer for on-the-go productivity.

## AI Accelerates Drug Discovery with Enveda's $311M Boost

Moving into a different kind of impact, AI is continuing to make waves in healthcare. Enveda, an AI biotech company, just secured a massive $311 million investment, bringing its valuation to $2 billion. They're using AI to accelerate the discovery of nature-derived drugs and are already testing treatments for skin conditions and even drugs designed to help preserve weight loss after stopping GLP-1s. This is a huge step forward in leveraging AI to find new, potentially life-changing medicines faster than ever before.

[Source: AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/enveda-secures-311m-to-bring-more-nature-derived-ai-drugs-into-clinical-trials/)

## Ema Raises $77M as AI Eats into Enterprise Software

Finally, AI's march into the enterprise world continues unabated. Ema, a company focused on AI for enterprise software and services, just raised another $77 million, bringing their total funding to $140 million. With over 50 enterprise customers, including giants like Google and Microsoft, Ema is a clear example of how AI isn't just a consumer-facing tool but is fundamentally reshaping how big businesses operate, making workflows more efficient and intelligent. This shows a growing trend of AI solutions directly impacting company bottom lines.
