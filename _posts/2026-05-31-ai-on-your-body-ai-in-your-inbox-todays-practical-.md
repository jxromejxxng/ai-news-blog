### Google's Gemini Spark: A Surprisingly Useful Assistant

Next, we're hearing good things about **Google's new 24/7 AI assistant, Gemini Spark**. One reviewer put it to the test and found it genuinely useful for automating everyday tasks.
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/30/i-put-googles-24-7-ai-assistant-gemini-spark-to-work-and-its-actually-pretty-useful/)

**How you might use it:** This isn't just another chatbot. Gemini Spark is designed to tackle practical tasks like summarizing your overflowing inbox, helping you plan local events, or even managing small bits of your schedule. Think of it as a proactive digital assistant that can sift through information and get things done for you, freeing up your mental bandwidth. While it's a bit puzzling why Google made this a separate product rather than integrating it directly into the main Gemini experience, the core takeaway is its utility. If you're constantly bogged down by administrative tasks, an always-on assistant like Spark could be a significant time-saver.
