### OpenAI Breaks Free: Expanding Beyond Azure to AWS

Big news for anyone building with OpenAI's models! OpenAI has struck a new deal with its biggest investor, Microsoft, that allows it to sell its products on Amazon Web Services (AWS). In return, Microsoft gets a bigger slice of OpenAI's revenue.

**Why you should care:** This is a win for flexibility and competition. If your company prefers AWS for its existing infrastructure, you'll soon have more direct access to OpenAI's cutting-edge models without being locked into Microsoft Azure. For businesses, this means more choice, potentially better pricing, and easier integration into your existing cloud environments. It could also spur more innovation as OpenAI's tools become more widely adopted across different platforms.

### A Billion-Dollar Bet on AI That Learns Differently

A former DeepMind researcher, David Silver, has just raised a staggering $1.1 billion for his new lab, Ineffable Intelligence. Their mission? To build AI that learns *without* human data.

**Why you should care:** This is a potentially huge leap for the future of AI. Most AIs today learn by sifting through massive amounts of human-generated data (text, images, code). This can lead to biases present in that data and limits what AI can learn if the data isn't available. If Silver's team succeeds, we could see AIs that are less biased, more efficient, and capable of learning in entirely new domains where human data is scarce. Think about AI for scientific discovery or complex simulations where there's no human precedent. This could unlock entirely new applications and capabilities for AI in the long run.

### The AI Phone is Coming: OpenAI & Skye Lead the Charge

Get ready for your phone to get a whole lot smarter. There are strong rumors that OpenAI is working on its own phone, potentially in collaboration with major chip and manufacturing partners. The big idea? AI agents that replace traditional apps. Meanwhile, a new AI home screen app for iPhone, Skye, is already attracting significant investor interest even before its launch.

**Why you should care:** This signals a major shift in how we interact with our mobile devices. Imagine a phone where you don't open a separate app for your calendar, email, or ride-sharing. Instead, an intelligent AI agent understands your intent and handles tasks across different services seamlessly. Need to book a flight and find a hotel? The AI agent could do it all for you with simple voice commands, pulling information from various sources without you ever opening a specific app. This could redefine productivity and convenience, making our phones feel less like a collection of tools and more like a truly intelligent assistant. Skye's app shows that even before a dedicated "AI phone," there's a strong appetite for making our current devices more "AI-aware."
