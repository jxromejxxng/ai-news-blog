### Google Cloud's AI-Fueled Growth Hits Capacity Limits

Google Cloud just hit a major milestone, surpassing $20 billion in quarterly revenue for the first time, largely driven by "surging demand for AI." However, there's a catch: they also mentioned that their growth was "capacity-constrained," meaning they could have grown even faster if they had more computing power available.

**Why you should care:** This is a big deal for anyone building or deploying AI. It shows the immense appetite for AI-related computing resources across industries. If you're looking to scale your AI projects, understand that the underlying infrastructure is in high demand, and securing that capacity is crucial. It also highlights the ongoing "arms race" among cloud providers to build out more AI-ready data centers to meet this exploding need.
