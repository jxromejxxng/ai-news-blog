## AI as a Mirror: The Pope's View on Power and Society

You might not expect the Pope to be a regular feature in an AI briefing, but Pope Leo XIV's first encyclical is making waves by using Artificial Intelligence as a lens to examine some much older, deeper societal issues.

According to TechCrunch, the encyclical isn't so much a direct critique of AI technology itself, but rather a profound diagnosis of existing problems like the concentration of power, the erosion of democratic processes, and how a small tech elite often shapes the world to its own advantage. AI, in this view, isn't the root cause of these issues, but rather a powerful magnifying glass, revealing and potentially accelerating them.

**Why this matters for you:**
For anyone building, deploying, or simply living with AI, this is a crucial reminder. It tells us to look beyond the technical capabilities of AI and consider the broader societal structures it operates within. Are our AI systems truly serving everyone, or are they inadvertently reinforcing existing power imbalances? For companies, this highlights the growing expectation for ethical AI development that considers its full societal footprint, not just its immediate utility or profit. It's a call to develop AI that contributes positively to a more equitable and democratic future, rather than just making existing problems more efficient.
