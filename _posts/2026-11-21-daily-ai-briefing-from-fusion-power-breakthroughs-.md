## Spotify Founder Backs AI for Clean Energy

First up, we're seeing some serious investment in AI's potential to solve big, global challenges. Daniel Ek, the visionary behind Spotify, is now throwing his weight behind Europa Fusion, a startup focused on, you guessed it, fusion power. What's cool here is that this isn't just any energy startup; it's founded by an AI researcher, suggesting that AI is playing a crucial role in accelerating the complex science needed to achieve commercially viable fusion. If they crack this, we're talking about a game-changer for clean, virtually limitless energy. It's a prime example of how AI isn't just about software, but about fundamentally changing how we approach scientific breakthroughs.

## AI Tackles a 167-Year-Old Math Mystery

Moving from practical energy to abstract mathematics, AI has apparently claimed a solution to the infamous 167-year-old Riemann hypothesis. For those not in the know, this is one of the most significant unsolved problems in mathematics, with a million-dollar prize attached! What makes this even more intriguing is that a human mathematician also presented a solution around the same time. This isn't just about AI showing off; it's a testament to its growing capability in complex problem-solving and fundamental research. Whether AI's solution holds up or not, it highlights how these tools can push the boundaries of human knowledge and serve as incredible accelerators for discovery, either by finding solutions or by inspiring new human approaches.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/ai-claimed-a-solution-to-the-167-year-old-riemann-hypothesis-and-a-human-did-too/)
