Hey everyone, here's a quick update on today's AI trends, focusing on what's practical and how these developments might change your daily grind or creative flow. We're seeing AI move beyond just answering questions to actively helping you get things done, plus a big leap in accessible content creation.

## AI Goes Beyond Chat: Your Apps Get Smarter

This is perhaps the biggest news for your day-to-day productivity. Google is expanding its "AI Mode" so it's not just a fancy chatbot anymore. Now, it can actually link up with and interact with your other apps. Think about that: instead of just *telling* you how to do something, your AI assistant could soon be *doing* it for you across your regular tools. This means less jumping between apps and more seamless task completion. Imagine asking your AI to "schedule a meeting with Sarah next Tuesday at 2 PM and send her the agenda from this document," and it just... does it. That's a huge step towards AI becoming a true digital assistant, not just an information provider.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/16/googles-ai-mode-now-lets-you-link-and-interact-with-select-apps/)

## Star in Your Own AI Videos with Google Vids

For all you content creators, marketers, or even just folks who want to make a quick personal video without getting on camera, Google Vids just got a major upgrade. You can now create videos starring a personalized AI avatar of yourself! Alongside the powerful Gemini Omni-powered tools for generating and editing videos from simple prompts or reference images, this means putting yourself "in" a video just got incredibly easy. Think about personalized marketing messages, quick explainers, or even just fun family updates, all with a digital you as the presenter. It's a game-changer for accessible, professional-looking video content.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/16/google-vids-now-lets-you-star-in-your-own-ai-videos/)

## Roblox Lowers the Bar for Game Creation

Ever wanted to make your own video game but felt overwhelmed by the technical hurdles? Roblox is making it a whole lot easier, especially for its massive mobile user base. They've launched a new "Build" feature that lets users generate basic games using just a single text prompt. This is huge for fostering creativity and getting more people into game design, even if it's just for fun. It democratizes game creation, allowing anyone with an idea to see it come to life quickly, without needing to learn complex coding or design tools from scratch.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/16/roblox-launches-an-ai-powered-game-creation-feature-in-its-mobile-app/)

## The Next Frontier: Visual AI is Getting Major Investment

Shifting gears a bit, it's always insightful to look at where the smart money is flowing in AI research. A former DeepMind researcher, Andrew Dai (who contributed to the foundational research for ChatGPT), just raised a whopping $300 million pre-seed valuation for a company focused on visual AI. This tells us that while text and language models have dominated the headlines, the ability for AI to understand, generate, and interact with the visual world is seen as the next major leap. Expect to see incredible advancements in how AI interprets images, videos, and even how it helps us create entirely new visual experiences in the near future.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/16/how-a-former-deepmind-researcher-raised-at-a-300m-pre-seed-valuation-before-launching-a-product/)
