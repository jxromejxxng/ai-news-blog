### **AI Agents Now Reviewing and Approving Your Code (No, Really!)**

If you're in software development, you know the bottleneck that code review can sometimes be. Well, Anthropic is tackling that head-on with **Claude Ship**. This new suite of developer tools lets AI agents not just *generate* code, but also review it, suggest fixes, and even *approve* code changes. Imagine the time saved and the routine grunt work eliminated! This means developers can focus on more complex, creative problems while AI handles the necessary but often tedious approval process.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/anthropic-lunches-claude-ship-letting-ai-agents-review-fix-and-approve-code-changes/)

### **Turn Your Market Predictions into a Portfolio with AI Agents**

Ever felt like you had a good sense of where the market was going but didn't know how to translate that into actual investments? A startup called **Supertake** just raised $20 million to let AI agents do exactly that. You provide your market predictions, and the AI agents will build and manage a stock portfolio based on your insights. It's an interesting concept that could potentially democratize active investing, allowing more people to put their financial intuitions to work without needing to become expert stock pickers themselves.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/supertake-raises-20m-to-let-ai-agents-turn-your-market-predictions-into-stock-portfolios/)

### **Google Search Gets Even More Personal, Tapping Your Drive and Wallet**

Google's AI Mode in Search is getting a significant upgrade: it can now pull information directly from your **Google Drive and Wallet** for more personalized responses. Need to find that receipt for your new gadget? Or details from a document you uploaded months ago? Google's AI will be able to access this personal data (with your permission, of course) to give you incredibly tailored answers. It's a big leap for convenience, making your digital life even more integrated, but also a reminder of how much data we entrust to these platforms.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/google-searchs-ai-mode-can-now-tap-into-drive-and-wallet-for-more-personalized-responses/)

### **Amazon Powers Up Its AI Infrastructure with a $7.5 Billion Acquisition**

In a massive move, **Amazon is acquiring Decart's AI optimization technology and research team** for a reported $7.5 billion. Why does this matter to you? Amazon is gearing up to sell its own custom AI training chips, called Trainium. By bringing Decart's expertise in-house, Amazon is significantly strengthening its ability to offer highly efficient and powerful AI infrastructure. This means better, faster, and more cost-effective AI services for businesses that rely on AWS, ultimately benefiting all of us who use AI-powered applications.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/amazon-snaps-up-decarts-ai-optimization-tech-for-7-5b-as-it-prepares-to-sell-trainium-chips/)

### **Customer Experience AI is Booming: Sierra Valued at $30 Billion**

The demand for AI in customer experience (CX) is absolutely exploding. **Sierra**, an enterprise CX company co-founded by former Salesforce co-CEO Bret Taylor, just doubled its valuation to an astonishing $30 billion in six months! They raised $1.75 billion in a Series F round. This massive investment highlights a clear trend: companies are pouring money into AI to make customer interactions smoother, faster, and more personalized. For us as consumers, this translates to better chatbots, quicker resolutions, and generally less frustrating customer service experiences.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/sierra-doubles-valuation-to-30b-within-six-months-as-demand-for-ai-in-cx-takes-off/)
