## AI Agents Are Officially Applying for Startup Jobs

Forget just using AI tools; we're now talking about AI agents becoming actual team members. At TechCrunch Disrupt 2026, leaders from Gusto, Insight Partners, and Leland discussed how early-stage companies are integrating AI agents right alongside their human employees. The big takeaway? It's not just possible, it's happening without sacrificing speed, accountability, or company culture. This means founders need to start thinking about how to onboard AI, assign tasks, and manage these new "colleagues" effectively. It's a fundamental shift in team building, and it's happening now.

## Your AI Agents Just Got Hands (on Your Smart Home)

This one's a game-changer for anyone with a smart home – or really, anyone interested in practical AI automation. Google is launching early access to a new server for Google Home that allows your favorite AI agents, like Claude and ChatGPT, to directly control your connected devices. Imagine telling your AI agent to turn off the lights, lock the doors, or even review your security camera summaries, all through natural language. This moves AI agents beyond just chatting and into actively managing your physical environment. For businesses, this hints at a future where AI agents can control and interact with various smart systems and IoT devices in offices, warehouses, or even manufacturing plants.
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/16/your-ai-agents-can-now-control-your-google-home-devices/)

## Anthropic Makes Claude Even More User-Friendly

Good news for users of Anthropic's Claude AI: they've merged the Claude chat experience with their "Cowork" interface. This means a more streamlined and efficient workflow for Pro and Max plan subscribers. For those of us who juggle multiple AI interactions, a unified interface is a big win. It simplifies how you interact with the AI, making it easier to leverage Claude's capabilities for tasks like drafting, research, and collaboration. It's a clear step towards making advanced AI models more integrated and intuitive for everyday productivity.

## Robotics is Still Waiting for Its "ChatGPT Moment"

While AI agents are making strides, the robotics industry is still looking for its breakthrough into widespread daily life. Nvidia's Les Karpas spoke at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026, explaining why robots haven't quite had their "ChatGPT moment" yet – that one big leap that makes them indispensable and ubiquitous. This is an important perspective. While we see amazing advancements in specific areas, the general-purpose, user-friendly robot that blends seamlessly into our homes and workplaces is still a future prospect. Understanding why this gap exists is crucial for anyone looking at investing in or developing robotics solutions.
