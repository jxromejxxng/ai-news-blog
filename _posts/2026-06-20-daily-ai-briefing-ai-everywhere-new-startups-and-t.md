### AI is Getting Woven into Everything, Everywhere

First up, if you're in India, get ready for AI to be a much bigger part of your digital world. Billionaire Mukesh Ambani's Reliance Industries is making a massive push to embed AI into pretty much every call, app, and home service they offer. With over 500 million telecom users, this isn't just a small experiment; it's a huge move to make AI a fundamental layer of daily digital interaction for a massive population. What does this mean for you? Expect smarter voice assistants, more personalized services, and AI-powered features popping up in the apps and devices you already use, making your digital life potentially smoother (or at least, different!).
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/19/billionaire-ambani-wants-ai-in-every-call-app-and-home/)

### Big Ideas (and Money) for New AI Ventures

Ever wondered what it takes to launch an AI startup these days? Apparently, a big idea and a strong founder can get you a long way, even before you've hired your team! The former CEO of Allbirds, Joey Zwillinger, has just secured a very large seed round for his new AI business. The catch? He's currently the sole employee. This isn't about a product you can use tomorrow, but it gives us a peek into the current AI investment landscape: investors are clearly eager to back ambitious AI visions, even if the execution plan is still in its nascent stages. It highlights the sheer excitement and potential they see in the AI space, proving that if you've got a compelling idea and the right connections, the sky's the limit for securing early funding.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/19/the-ceo-of-allbirds-new-ai-biz-has-a-plan-but-no-employees/)

### Want Less AI? How to Turn Off Google Docs' Gemini Pop-ups

Now for something directly practical for many of you. If you've been using Google Docs lately, you've probably noticed those "write with Gemini" pop-ups appearing, suggesting AI-generated text. While these can be helpful for some, not everyone wants AI suggestions constantly interrupting their flow. Good news: you can turn them off! TechCrunch has a quick guide on how to disable these features, giving you back full control over your writing experience. It's a great reminder that as AI gets integrated into more of our everyday tools, having the option to customize or opt-out is crucial for a comfortable user experience.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/18/how-to-turn-off-ai-in-your-google-docs/)
