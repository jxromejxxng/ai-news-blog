## Engineers: Still the Backbone, Not the Bottleneck

Remember all the talk about AI coming for engineering jobs? Well, the latest data suggests a different story. While layoffs have certainly hit the tech sector, engineers are actually making up a larger share of new hires. This isn't just about building AI, but about integrating it, maintaining systems, and solving complex problems that AI can assist with, but not fully replace. It seems the demand for skilled problem-solvers who can leverage AI as a powerful tool is only growing. So, if you're in engineering or considering it, don't sweat it – focus on adapting and integrating new tech.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/24/ai-was-supposed-to-kill-engineering-jobs-but-new-data-suggests-theyre-the-most-resilient/)

## The Era of Token Rationing: Companies Get Smart About AI Costs

This one's a big deal for anyone using AI tools in a corporate setting. For a while, some employees were "tokenmaxxing"—using AI for every tiny task, racking up significant costs for their companies. Think of it like a printer budget, but for AI prompts. That brief era is over. Companies are now scrambling to implement "token rationing" to manage their AI budgets. This means we'll likely see more thoughtful, strategic use of AI within organizations, and employees will need to be more deliberate about when and how they leverage these powerful (and sometimes pricey) tools. It's a reminder that while AI is incredible, it's not free, and businesses are quickly learning to optimize its usage.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/24/companies-are-scrambling-to-stop-employees-from-maxing-out-ai-budgets-with-small-tasks/)

## Facebook's New AI Companion for Creators

Good news for content creators out there! Facebook is rolling out a new AI companion app specifically designed to help creators. This app will integrate Facebook's recently launched AI creator assistant, aiming to streamline workflows, generate ideas, and generally make the lives of creators easier. While it's currently in testing with select creators, this is a clear signal that major platforms are investing heavily in AI to empower their user base, especially those driving content. Keep an eye out for this if you're a creator looking for an extra edge.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/24/facebook-rolls-out-an-ai-companion-app-for-creators/)

## Figma Levels Up with AI, Code Layers, and Animations

Designers and developers, pay attention! Figma, the collaborative design powerhouse, just dropped a major update that's going to change how you work. They're adding new code layers, support for motion and shaders, and—most importantly for our briefing—the ability to create custom plug-ins for various tasks using AI. This means designers can potentially automate more repetitive tasks, generate variations, or even bridge the gap between design and development more smoothly. It's another fantastic example of how AI isn't replacing creative tools but enhancing them, making them more powerful and efficient.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/24/figma-adds-code-layers-support-for-animations-more-ai-features-in-new-update/)
