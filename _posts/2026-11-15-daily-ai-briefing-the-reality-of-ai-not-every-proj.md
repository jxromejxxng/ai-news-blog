## The AI Graveyard: Learning from What Didn't Make It

We often hear about the groundbreaking successes in AI, but what about the projects that fizzle out or completely miss the mark? A recent piece from TechCrunch shines a light on the "AI graveyard," a growing list of projects and startups that didn't quite make it. This isn't just about small, unknown ventures failing; even tech giants like Apple have faced significant delays and struggles with their AI ambitions, like the ongoing evolution (or lack thereof) of Siri.

OpenAI, a name synonymous with AI breakthroughs, also reportedly had a messy launch attempt for a "super app" that didn't live up to expectations. What does this tell us? It's a stark reminder that AI development is incredibly complex, expensive, and far from a guaranteed success. For businesses, this means approaching AI with a healthy dose of realism. Don't jump on every hype train. Instead, focus on clear problems you want to solve, start small, and be prepared for iterative development and potential setbacks. Even the best ideas need solid execution, and sometimes, the market just isn't ready, or the tech isn't quite there yet.
