## The AI Graveyard: Not Every Idea Sticks

First up, a dose of reality. While AI seems to be everywhere, it's not all smooth sailing. A recent report highlights "The AI Graveyard," a list of projects and startups that have either shut down or seriously missed expectations. **Why this matters to you:** This isn't about doom and gloom; it's about understanding the real world of innovation. For companies, it's a reminder that even with massive resources, building truly impactful AI is hard. For individuals, it's a good reminder to be discerning – not every flashy AI announcement will stick around. Innovation is messy, and failures are part of the process.

## AI's Political Leanings: Grok vs. OpenAI

Now, for something that definitely needs your attention if you're using AI for information or content generation: a new study has revealed some clear political biases in prominent AI models. When "forced to pick" candidates in nearly 500 election races, Grok's AI overwhelmingly recommended Republican candidates, while OpenAI's GPT models favored Democrats. The two models disagreed in over 90% of these races!

**Why this matters to you:** This is a huge deal, especially with elections always on the horizon. If you're using AI to research candidates, draft political content, or even just get general information, you need to be acutely aware that these tools aren't neutral. They reflect the biases present in their training data and the choices made by their developers.

**Practical takeaway:** Always cross-reference information, especially on sensitive topics like politics. Don't rely solely on one AI model for your news or perspectives. Understand that AI, like any information source, has a point of view, whether intentional or not. Critical thinking remains your best tool.
[Source: TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/24/forced-to-pick-groks-ai-model-chooses-republicans-openais-picks-democrats/)

## Your Next Texting Buddy: AI Agents in Your Messages

On a more immediately practical note, AI agents are increasingly making their way into our text messages. Forget downloading a new app for every little thing; these AI assistants can live right where you already communicate. We're seeing everything from general-purpose assistants that can answer questions or set reminders, to specialized agents designed for families, travel planning, or work tasks.

**How you can use this:** Imagine an AI helping you coordinate a family dinner, finding the best flight deals, or summarizing meeting notes, all by just sending a text. These agents are designed to be seamless and integrated into your daily communication flow. Keep an eye out for these – they could be a super convenient way to offload small tasks and get information without leaving your messaging app. It's about making AI work for you, right where you are.
