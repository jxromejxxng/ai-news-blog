## Shopify Shows AI Drives Sales, Not Just Clicks

Good news for e-commerce businesses! **Shopify is reporting that its AI search features are not only driving more traffic but also tripling sales year-over-year in Q2.** This is a practical example of AI directly impacting the bottom line. It also pushes back against the fear that AI will simply "cannibalize" existing search traffic. Instead, it's acting as an accelerator, helping customers find what they need faster and boosting conversions. For any business owner, this is a clear sign that integrating smart AI tools can lead to tangible growth.
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/shopify-says-ai-search-is-driving-more-traffic-and-sales-not-replacing-google/)

## Anthropic Makes a Big Bet on Custom AI Hardware

We often talk about AI models, but what about the hardware they run on? **Anthropic, the company behind the Claude AI model, is building its own team to design custom AI chips.** This is a significant strategic move. By co-designing hardware and models, they aim to make their AI run faster and more efficiently. This trend of major AI players developing their own silicon is crucial because it means more powerful, specialized, and energy-efficient AI in the long run. It’s about owning the entire stack to push the boundaries of what AI can do.
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/anthropic-is-hiring-an-ai-chip-design-team/)

## Local AI for Your Apps? MacPaw Teams Up for On-Device Inference

Finally, for developers focused on user experience and privacy, **MacPaw is partnering with Liquid AI to bring on-device inference to developers building for its app store.** This means AI models can run directly on a user's device rather than relying on cloud servers. The practical benefits are huge: faster performance, reduced latency, and enhanced privacy since data doesn't need to leave the device. For app developers, this opens up new possibilities for creating smarter, more responsive, and more secure applications.
