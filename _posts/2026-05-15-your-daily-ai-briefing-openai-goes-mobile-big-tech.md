## OpenAI's Codex is Coming to Your Phone

Get ready to code on the go! OpenAI is bringing its powerful Codex model, which can understand and generate code, to your phone. This is a big deal for developers and anyone who needs to manage workflows with enhanced flexibility. Imagine debugging or drafting code snippets right from your mobile device – that's a serious boost to productivity and convenience.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/14/openai-says-codex-is-coming-to-your-phone/)

## OpenAI Reportedly Gearing Up for Legal Action Against Apple

In a surprising turn of events, OpenAI is apparently so frustrated with Apple over a ChatGPT integration that didn't meet their expectations for subscribers and prominence, they're considering legal action. This isn't the first time an Apple partner has felt burned, and it highlights the complex and sometimes contentious relationships between big tech players as AI becomes central to everyone's strategy. This could set a precedent for how AI companies negotiate their integration into major platforms.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/14/openai-is-reportedly-preparing-legal-action-against-apple-it-wouldnt-be-the-first-partner-to-feel-burned/)

## Cerebras Kicks Off 2026 IPO Season with a Bang

Remember Cerebras? The AI chipmaker that seemed to be struggling a year ago? Well, they just raised a massive $5.5 billion and saw their stock pop an incredible 108% in what's being called the first huge tech IPO of 2026. This is a strong signal that investor confidence in core AI infrastructure and specialized hardware is soaring. For companies, this means more capital flowing into the development of powerful AI training and inference systems, which could lead to even more advanced tools down the line.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/14/cerebras-raises-5-5b-kicking-off-2026s-ipo-season-with-a-bang/)

## Cisco Cuts Jobs to Double Down on AI

Cisco, a networking giant, has announced nearly 4,000 job cuts, despite reporting "record quarterly revenue." The reason? To free up resources and spend more on AI. This is a stark reminder of how AI is reshaping corporate priorities. Companies are making tough decisions to pivot towards AI investments, and while it's unfortunate news for those affected, it underscores the strategic importance and perceived necessity of AI integration for future growth and competitiveness across all industries.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/14/cisco-cuts-nearly-4000-jobs-to-spend-more-on-ai-reports-record-quarterly-revenue/)
