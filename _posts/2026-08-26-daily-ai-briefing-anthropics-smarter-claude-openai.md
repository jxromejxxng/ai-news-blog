### **Anthropic's Claude Finally Remembers Your Conversations**

This is a huge quality-of-life upgrade for anyone using Claude for ongoing projects. Anthropic is rolling out a shared memory feature for Claude Cowork. What does this mean for you? No more having to re-brief your AI assistant on past conversations, project details, or your specific preferences every time you open a new chat or switch contexts. Claude will now remember what you've told it across different interactions within the Cowork app. Think of it like having a colleague who actually pays attention and doesn't make you repeat yourself. This saves time and makes your AI interactions much more fluid and efficient.

### **OpenAI's "Jalapeño" Chip Promises Faster, More Efficient AI**

OpenAI is making waves not just with its models, but also with the hardware that runs them. Their new custom chip, codenamed "Jalapeño," is designed for "fast inference at scale." In plain English, this means it's built to quickly process AI requests and deliver responses, especially when handling a massive number of users simultaneously. Benchmarks show Jalapeño outperforming current state-of-the-art options in terms of tokens per user and throughput per kilowatt. For businesses, this is significant: it could lead to more responsive AI services, lower operational costs for running AI models, and the ability to serve more users with the same infrastructure. Essentially, faster, cheaper, and more scalable AI.
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/25/openais-jalapeno-chip-is-built-for-fast-inference-at-scale-benchmarks-show/)

### **Stability AI Secures $76 Million in Fresh Funding**

Stability AI, the company behind the popular open-source image generator Stable Diffusion, has successfully raised another $76 million. This brings their total funding to an impressive $232 million. For those of us who use or follow creative AI, this is great news. More funding means Stability AI can continue to innovate, improve its models, and potentially expand its offerings. Given their commitment to open-source development, this investment could fuel further advancements in accessible AI tools for artists, designers, and developers worldwide. It underscores the continued investor confidence in the generative AI space, especially for companies fostering community-driven innovation.

### **Keenable is Building a Dedicated Web Index for AI Agents**

Imagine AI assistants that can not only understand your requests but also proactively gather and synthesize information from the entire web, just like a human researcher. That's the promise of AI agents, and a new startup called Keenable is building a foundational piece for them. Exiting stealth mode with a $26 million seed round, Keenable is creating a vast web search index specifically optimized for AI agents. Why is this important? Current search engines are built for humans. An index designed for AI means agents can more efficiently and accurately find, understand, and utilize information, making them far more capable and reliable for complex tasks – from market research to personal assistance. This is a key step towards truly intelligent, autonomous AI tools.
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/25/accel-backed-keenable-is-indexing-the-web-for-ai-agents/)
