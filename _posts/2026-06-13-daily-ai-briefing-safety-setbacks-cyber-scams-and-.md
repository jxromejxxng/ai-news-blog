### Google Sues AI-Powered Cybercrime Ring

On the darker side of AI, Google is taking legal action against a Chinese cybercrime operation called "Outsider Enterprise." This group allegedly used AI to scam hundreds of thousands of victims, sending a staggering 2.5 million text messages in just two weeks.

**Why this matters:** For all of us, this is a serious wake-up call. AI can make scams incredibly effective, personalized, and hard to detect at scale. Always be vigilant about unsolicited messages, suspicious links, and remember that advanced tech isn't just for good guys. For businesses, this underscores the urgent need to bolster your cybersecurity defenses and educate your employees and customers about AI-powered phishing and fraud.
