## Smarter Browsing with Chrome's New AI Skills

First up, Google is making your daily browsing a whole lot smarter by adding "Skills" to Chrome. Imagine you have a complex prompt you use often for summarizing articles, drafting emails, or generating ideas across different websites. Now, instead of retyping or copy-pasting, you can save these AI prompts as "Skills" and reuse them with a click. This builds on Gemini's existing browser integration and is a fantastic step towards making AI a seamless, personalized part of your workflow. Less friction, more productivity – that's what we like to see!
[Source: AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/14/google-adds-ai-skills-to-chrome-to-help-you-save-favorite-workflows/)

## ChatGPT is Getting Smarter with Your Money

Big news on the personal finance front! OpenAI, the company behind ChatGPT, has acquired Hiro, an AI personal finance startup. This move strongly suggests that OpenAI is building financial planning capabilities directly into ChatGPT. Think about it: getting AI assistance with budgeting, investment insights, or even just understanding complex financial jargon could become a standard feature. While it's early days, this acquisition signals a clear intent to make AI a more direct and powerful tool for managing your personal economy.
[Source: AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/13/openai-has-bought-ai-personal-finance-startup-hiro/)

## Brain Sensors on the Horizon for Healing

This next one is straight out of science fiction, but it's happening! Max Hodak’s Science Corp. is preparing to place its first sensor in a human brain. The goal? To help address a range of neurological conditions. One potential early use could be delivering gentle electrical stimulation to damaged brain or spinal cord cells to encourage healing. This is a monumental step in neurotech, offering hope for significant advancements in treating injuries and conditions that have historically been incredibly challenging. It's a reminder that AI's impact isn't just digital; it's also deeply physical and life-changing.
[Source: AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/14/max-hodaks-science-corp-is-preparing-to-place-its-first-sensor-in-a-human-brain/)

## How AI Agents are Fueling Business Growth

While many companies are still figuring out how to adapt to the AI era, some are absolutely thriving. Vercel, a 10-year-old dev tool and website hosting platform, is a great example. Their CEO, Guillermo Rauch, is signaling IPO readiness, largely thanks to the explosion of AI-generated apps and agents. This shows that the infrastructure and tools supporting AI development are becoming incredibly valuable. For businesses, this is a clear signal: investing in platforms that enable AI innovation, particularly around AI agents, can lead to significant revenue surges and market advantage.
[Source: AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/13/vercel-ceo-guillermo-rauch-signals-ipo-readiness-as-ai-agents-fuel-revenue-surge/)
