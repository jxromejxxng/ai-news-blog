## Users Push Back on Google's AI Search Overhaul

Google's ambitious overhaul of its Search at I/O 2026, which swapped out those familiar blue links for AI agents, has hit a snag. It turns out, not everyone wants their search results curated entirely by AI, leading to a swift backlash. Privacy-focused browser DuckDuckGo saw its app installs jump by a significant 30% as users looked for alternatives to what they felt was being 'force-fed' Google’s AI Search. This is a clear signal that even with cutting-edge tech, user preference and control remain paramount. For us, it's a reminder that we have choices in how we interact with AI, and for big tech, it's a lesson in gentle integration.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/26/duckduckgo-installs-are-up-30-as-users-reject-being-force-fed-googles-ai-search/)

## The Human Element in Robot Training

Ever wondered how robots learn about the real world? It often involves a lot of human effort. A new startup called Human Archive, born from UC Berkeley and Stanford research, is tapping into India’s vibrant gig economy to gather crucial physical training data for AI and robotics labs worldwide. They're paying gig workers to wear camera-equipped caps and sensor devices, essentially collecting real-world footage and data that helps AI systems understand our physical environment. This highlights the often-unseen human labor that powers even the most advanced AI, creating new opportunities in the gig economy while fueling the next generation of intelligent machines.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/26/human-archive-taps-into-indias-services-startups-to-collect-data-for-physical-ai/)

## Music Giants vs. Unauthorized AI Music

The battle for intellectual property rights in the age of AI continues. Universal Music Group (UMG) and TikTok have renewed their agreement, specifically targeting the combat of unauthorized AI-generated music. For years, UMG has been a vocal advocate, pushing streaming services, platforms, and AI companies to implement stricter content moderation policies to protect artists and their work from misuse by AI. This partnership underscores the growing importance of clear guidelines and robust systems to ensure that while AI offers incredible creative potential, it doesn't come at the cost of artists' livelihoods and rights.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/26/universal-music-group-and-tiktok-renew-agreement-to-combat-unauthorized-ai-music/)
