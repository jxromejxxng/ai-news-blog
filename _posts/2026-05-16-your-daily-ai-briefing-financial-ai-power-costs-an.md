## AI's Growing Appetite for Power is Driving Up Costs

Here's a practical one that might hit your wallet, or at least your company's budget: AI is driving up electricity demand, and that's translating into higher energy prices. Lake Tahoe, a popular vacation spot, is seeing this firsthand. For businesses running significant AI workloads, this means operational costs are on the rise. It's a stark reminder that advanced tech isn't just lines of code; it has real-world physical infrastructure and resource demands we need to account for.

## ChatGPT Gets Personal with Your Finances

Big news for your personal finances: OpenAI has launched a ChatGPT version specifically for managing money, and yes, it will let you connect your bank accounts. Imagine a dashboard showing your portfolio, spending, subscriptions, and upcoming payments all in one place, powered by AI. This could be a game-changer for personal financial management, offering insights and organization that usually require multiple apps or manual tracking. Of course, the immediate question for many will be security and privacy when linking sensitive financial data, so make sure to do your research before diving in.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-personal-finance-will-let-you-connect-bank-accounts/)

## Runway Aims to Outpace Google in AI Video Generation

Remember Runway, the company that started by helping filmmakers? Well, they're now aiming to beat Google in the AI video space. Their bet is that advanced video generation is key to building "world models"—AI that understands and simulates complex environments. For creators and businesses in media, this signals intense competition and rapid innovation, meaning even more powerful and accessible video creation tools are on the horizon. Being an "outsider" might just be the advantage they need to innovate faster.

## Osaurus Brings Hybrid AI Models to Your Mac

For Mac users, Osaurus is a new app that offers the best of both worlds: local and cloud AI models. This means you can leverage powerful cloud AI when you need it, but also keep your sensitive memory, files, and tools running on your own hardware. This is a big win for privacy and control, allowing users to decide where their data is processed, and potentially offering faster performance for certain tasks without relying solely on an internet connection.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/osaurus-brings-both-local-and-cloud-ai-models-to-your-mac/)
