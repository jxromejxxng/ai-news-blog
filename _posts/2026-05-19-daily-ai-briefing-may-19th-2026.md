## AI for Drug Discovery Gets an Easy Button

Imagine being able to leverage cutting-edge AI for complex tasks like drug discovery without needing a PhD in computer science. That's exactly what's happening as **SandboxAQ brings its powerful drug discovery models directly to Anthropic's Claude AI**.

This is huge for the pharmaceutical and biotech industries. Previously, only companies with massive R&D budgets and specialized AI teams could really tap into these advanced models. Now, by integrating with a user-friendly platform like Claude, more researchers and smaller firms can experiment, analyze, and accelerate their drug development processes. It's all about democratizing access to powerful tools, making it easier for brilliant minds to focus on the science, not just the underlying AI infrastructure.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/18/sandboxaq-brings-its-drug-discovery-models-to-claude-no-phd-in-computing-required/)

## Anthropic Powers Up for Developers with New Acquisition

Speaking of Claude, its creator, Anthropic, is making moves to solidify its position in the AI ecosystem. They've just **acquired Stainless, a dev tools startup** known for automating the creation and maintenance of Software Development Kits (SDKs).

For those not in the weeds of development, an SDK is essentially a toolkit that makes it easier for other software developers to build applications that connect with a particular service (like Claude's AI). By bringing Stainless in-house, Anthropic is making a clear statement: they want to make it as straightforward as possible for businesses and developers to integrate Claude into their own products and workflows. This means less friction, faster development cycles, and ultimately, more innovative AI applications powered by Claude. If your company is building with AI, this acquisition signals a smoother road ahead for integrating with Anthropic's models.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/18/anthropic-has-acquired-the-dev-tools-startup-used-by-openai-google-and-cloudflare/)

## The Future is Looking Glassy: AI in Your Eyewear

Moving from software to hardware, we're seeing tangible progress in how AI will integrate into our daily lives. South Korean startup **LetinAR is making waves with its tiny, thumbnail-sized lenses** designed to be the optical backbone of future AI glasses.

This isn't just about fancy smart glasses; it's about making AI ubiquitous and context-aware. Imagine AI assistants that can overlay information directly onto your view of the world, provide real-time translations, or even help with complex tasks by showing you instructions right in front of your eyes. For professionals, this could mean hands-free access to data, remote assistance, or enhanced situational awareness. For everyday life, it promises a more seamless blend of digital information with our physical reality. LetinAR's focus on compact, efficient optics is a critical step toward making these AI glasses practical and comfortable for everyone.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/18/south-koreas-letinar-is-building-the-optics-behind-ai-glasses/)
