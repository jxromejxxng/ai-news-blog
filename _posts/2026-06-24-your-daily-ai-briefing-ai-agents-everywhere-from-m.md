Hey everyone, here's a quick update on today's AI trends and what they mean for how we work and interact. We're seeing a clear theme emerging: AI agents are getting more specialized and integrated into our daily workflows. Let's dive in!

### Personalized Marketing at Scale with AI Agents

First up, we've got an interesting move from India's MoEngage. They're making a big bet that the future of marketing isn't just about general AI, but about **millions of individual AI agents**. Think about it: an AI agent assigned to *each* of your customers. The idea here is hyper-personalization on a massive scale. Instead of sending out broad campaigns, these agents could learn individual customer preferences, behaviors, and even predict their needs to deliver incredibly tailored messages and offers. For businesses, this means potentially much higher engagement and conversion rates. For us as consumers, it could mean more relevant communication, but also raises questions about how much data is being used and how these interactions will feel.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/23/indias-moengage-bets-marketings-future-on-millions-of-ai-agents/)

### Your Always-On AI Teammate in Slack

Next, let's talk about something that could fundamentally change how teams collaborate. Anthropic, the creators of the Claude AI, are rolling out "Claude Tag" into Slack. Imagine an **always-on AI teammate** living right there in your company's Slack channels. Claude Tag isn't just about answering quick questions; it's designed to learn your company's unique context, institutional knowledge, and enterprise workflows by observing conversations. This means it could help new hires get up to speed faster, summarize long discussions, find specific information buried in past chats, or even draft responses based on your company's tone. It's a huge play for Anthropic to embed itself deep into organizational operations, capturing that invaluable company-specific knowledge. Productivity boost? Absolutely. But also, a significant step in AI becoming a core part of your team's memory and daily operations.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/23/anthropics-claude-tag-is-learning-your-company-one-slack-message-at-a-time/)

### AI Agents Stepping into the Hiring Process

Lastly, let's look at how AI is shaking up the job market. Startup Fika Jobs just raised $4 million to build a **video-first hiring platform where AI agents interview candidates**. Picture this: a platform that feels like a mix between LinkedIn and TikTok. Candidates create short video profiles, and AI agents conduct initial interviews. For job seekers, this could mean a more engaging way to present themselves beyond a resume, and potentially a more objective initial screening process. For companies, it offers a way to screen a larger pool of candidates more efficiently, potentially reducing unconscious bias in early stages and allowing human recruiters to focus on the most promising candidates. It's a fresh take on how technology can streamline and even humanize (in a strange way) the often-clunky hiring process.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/23/fika-jobs-raises-4m-to-build-a-video-first-hiring-platform-where-ai-agents-interview-candidates/)

### The Takeaway

What ties these stories together is the growing prevalence and specialization of **AI agents**. We're moving beyond general-purpose AI tools to highly focused agents designed to tackle specific problems, whether it's personalizing your marketing, understanding your company's internal workings, or streamlining job interviews. These tools aren't just automating tasks; they're learning, adapting, and integrating themselves into the very fabric of how businesses operate and how we interact with technology. It's an exciting, and sometimes challenging, shift in how we think about productivity and intelligent assistance.
