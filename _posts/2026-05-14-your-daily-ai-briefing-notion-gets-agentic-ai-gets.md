## Your Workspace Just Got Smarter (and More Agentic!)

First up, if you're a Notion user, get ready for a significant upgrade. **Notion just rolled out a new developer platform that lets you connect AI agents, external data, and custom code directly into your workspace.** This is a huge step towards "agentic productivity software," meaning AI isn't just a fancy spellchecker anymore; it can actually take action and integrate information from across your tools. Think about having an AI that pulls sales data from a CRM, summarizes it, and drafts a report in Notion, all without you lifting a finger. This could be a game-changer for teams looking to automate more complex workflows. [AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/13/notion-just-turned-its-workspace-into-a-hub-for-ai-agents/)

## AI That Anticipates Your Needs

Ever wish your tech could just *know* what you need before you even ask? That future is getting closer. **Anthropic's head of product for Claude Code and Cowork, Cat Wu, says the next big step for AI is proactivity** – meaning AI that anticipates your needs. We're already seeing this vision come to life with new tools like **Poppy, a proactive AI assistant that connects to your calendar, email, and messages to surface reminders, suggestions, and tasks.** Imagine an AI that sees you have a meeting tomorrow, flags relevant documents, and suggests follow-up actions, all automatically. This isn't just about saving time; it's about reducing mental load. [AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/13/anthropics-cat-wu-says-that-in-the-future-ai-will-anticipate-your-needs-before-you-know-what-they-are/) [AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/13/poppy-debuts-a-proactive-ai-assistant-to-help-organize-your-digital-life/)

## Amazon's New AI Shopping Buddy

Speaking of anticipating needs, your shopping experience just got an AI upgrade. **Amazon has launched a new AI shopping assistant for its search bar, powered by Alexa+.** This means whether you're on your phone, desktop, or an Echo Show, you'll get more personalized recommendations and a more automated shopping experience, not just on Amazon but across other online retailers too. If you've ever wished online shopping was a bit smarter about what you *really* want, this is a step in that direction. [AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/13/amazon-launches-an-ai-shopping-assistant-for-the-search-bar-powered-by-alexa/)

## Anthropic Edges Out OpenAI in Business Customers

In a significant shift for the AI industry, new data suggests **Anthropic now has more business customers than OpenAI.** According to a survey compiled from fintech firm Ramp’s clients’ expense data, 34.4% of participating businesses are paying for Anthropic services, compared to 32.3% for OpenAI. This indicates a growing preference for Anthropic's offerings, particularly its Claude models, among businesses. It's a clear signal that the competition in the enterprise AI space is heating up, and companies are carefully evaluating their options beyond the initial market leader. [AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/13/anthropic-now-has-more-business-customers-than-openai-according-to-ramp-data/)

## Privacy for Your AI Chats: WhatsApp Adds Incognito Mode

Finally, a welcome update for privacy-conscious users. **WhatsApp has added an incognito mode for Meta AI chats.** This means that when you use this mode, your conversations are not saved, and messages will disappear by default once you close the chat. As AI becomes more integrated into our communication tools, having options for privacy and ephemerality is crucial. It’s good to see major platforms like WhatsApp giving users more control over their data in AI interactions.
