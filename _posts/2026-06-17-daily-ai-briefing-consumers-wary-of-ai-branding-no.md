## Consumers Are Side-Eyeing 'AI' in Your Branding

First up, a big one for anyone in marketing or product development: A new survey by WordPress VIP found that a whopping 60% of US consumers are actually *turned off* by the word "AI" in brand messaging. This is a crucial insight, especially as companies are increasingly looking at AI-powered search as a referral channel. It seems that while we're excited about what AI can *do*, we're not necessarily keen on being told it's "AI-powered!" It's a reminder that focusing on the *benefit* (e.g., "smarter search," "personalized recommendations") rather than just the technology itself is key. People want solutions, not just buzzwords.

## Plaud Hits $100 Million: AI Notetakers Are Big Business

Remember those AI meeting notetakers we've talked about? Well, Plaud, a company in that crowded space, just announced its software business has topped $100 million in Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) after shipping over 2 million AI notetakers. This isn't just a niche gadget anymore; it's a testament to how specific, problem-solving AI tools can create massive value. If you're still manually taking notes in meetings or struggling to keep track of action items, this kind of tech is clearly proving its worth in the market. It's a great example of AI making a tangible impact on daily productivity.

## The Urgent Quest for More Reliable AI

One of AI's biggest challenges – and a major frustration for users – is its tendency to "hallucinate" or make factual errors. That's why news about companies like Probably raising $9 million to build a more reliable kind of AI is so important. Their goal? To prevent those factual errors from reaching users and achieve accuracy on par with deterministic systems. For anyone using AI for critical tasks, or for businesses looking to integrate AI deeper into their operations, this push for reliability is fundamental. It's about building trust and ensuring AI isn't just fast, but also right.
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/16/probably-raises-9m-to-build-a-more-reliable-kind-of-ai/)

## ChatGPT's Market Dominance Isn't Absolute Anymore

For the first time, ChatGPT's market share has slipped below 50%. While it still reigns as the most popular AI assistant globally with over 1.1 billion monthly users, competitors like Gemini (662 million) and Claude (245 million) are rapidly gaining ground. What does this mean for you? More competition usually leads to more innovation and better features for users. If you've been exclusively using ChatGPT, now might be a good time to explore what other AI assistants like Gemini or Claude have to offer. For businesses, this diversification means more options for integrating AI, and a need to stay updated on the best tool for specific tasks.
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/16/chatgpts-market-share-slips-below-50-for-first-time/)

## AI Agents Are Revolutionizing Customer Service

Finally, let's look at Respond.io, a Malaysian startup that just raised $62.5 million to expand its AI agent-powered messaging app. What's interesting here is that they use AI agents to handle high volumes of customer inquiries and, crucially, charge *per conversation*, not per seat. This model could be a game-changer for businesses, especially those with fluctuating customer service needs. It highlights how AI isn't just for automating simple FAQs; it's capable of managing complex customer interactions at scale, potentially making customer service more efficient and cost-effective. For any business looking to streamline operations and improve customer experience, AI agents are definitely a space to watch.
