### Taking Back Control: Taming AI in Your Google Docs

First up, a super practical one for many of us. If you've been using Google Docs lately, you've probably noticed those "write with Gemini" pop-ups. While helpful for some, they can be a bit much if you're just trying to get your work done without AI suggestions constantly in your face. Good news: you can turn them off! This is a great reminder that while AI is integrating everywhere, we still have agency over how and when we use it in our daily tools. It's about making AI work for *you*, not the other way around.

### The AI ROI Reality Check: From "Tokenmaxxing" to Cost Management

Remember when "tokenmaxxing" was the buzzword in Silicon Valley? Companies were pushing employees to use AI as much as possible, just to see what would stick. Well, the bill has arrived, and it's a big one for many. We're hearing stories of companies like Uber blowing through their annual AI budget in a few months, others cutting back on expensive AI licenses, and even Meta reportedly killing internal AI usage leaderboards.

This isn't a sign that AI isn't valuable, but rather a crucial moment of reckoning for enterprises. It's shifting from experimental usage to a focus on **Return on Investment (ROI)**. Businesses are now asking: Where is AI truly adding value? How can we optimize its use to justify the significant costs? If you're in a company exploring or implementing AI, this is your cue to start thinking strategically about budget, measurable outcomes, and efficient integration, rather than just raw usage.

### Global Concerns: Who Controls the AI Off Switch?

Here's a big one with geopolitical implications. At the recent G7 summit, leaders like French President Macron and Indian PM Modi voiced serious concerns. They want access to powerful American AI models, but they're worried the U.S. could suddenly cut off that access – a fear made very real by recent service blackouts, like one from Anthropic.

For businesses operating globally, this highlights a critical point about **supply chain risk and digital sovereignty**. Relying heavily on AI services from a single nation or provider could expose you to significant operational risks if political tensions or technical issues lead to a cutoff. This conversation will likely accelerate efforts for AI development in more countries and push for open standards or diversified AI partnerships. It's not just about what AI can do, but who controls it.
[Source: AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/17/world-leaders-want-american-ai-they-just-dont-want-america-to-be-able-to-turn-it-off/)

### Public Perception: A Muted Optimism for AI's Impact

While Wall Street might be bullish on AI, everyday Americans are far less optimistic. A new Pew Research study shows that only 16% of Americans believe AI will have a positive impact on society. This is a pretty stark contrast and an important data point.

Why does this matter? For companies developing AI, it means that building trust and demonstrating tangible, positive impacts is more critical than ever. For individuals, it reflects a healthy skepticism and a desire to understand AI's real-world implications beyond the hype. This low public optimism could influence everything from consumer adoption rates to future regulations. We're in a phase where AI needs to prove its worth to the general public, not just to investors.
[Source: AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/17/only-16-percent-of-americans-think-ai-will-have-a-positive-impact-on-society-a-new-study-shows/)

### Pinterest Gets Conversational with 'Ask Pinterest'

On a lighter, more consumer-focused note, Pinterest is jumping into the AI game with an experimental shopping app called 'Ask Pinterest'. This new tool lets users chat with an AI to get recommendations and inspiration for their shopping needs.

This is a great example of how AI is being integrated into specific, everyday tasks to make them easier and more intuitive. Think about it: instead of endless scrolling, you can just ask "Ask Pinterest" for ideas for a summer backyard party or a unique gift for a friend. It's a practical application that directly enhances the user experience, moving beyond simple search to conversational discovery. Keep an eye out for more platforms integrating this kind of personalized, AI-powered assistance.
