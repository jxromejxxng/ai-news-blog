### ChatGPT Can Now Text For You

Ever wish you had a personal assistant to handle your messages? Well, ChatGPT just got a new trick that's pretty close! Thanks to a new Apple Messages integration, ChatGPT can now act as your automated text scribe. Imagine dictating a message to ChatGPT, and it not only drafts it perfectly but also sends it directly through your Apple Messages app. This is a huge step for hands-free communication and productivity, making your AI assistant even more integrated into your daily life.

Here's a stat that might make you pause: a recent study found that a third of all web pages published since ChatGPT's launch show signs of AI authorship. That's a massive shift! AI models are no longer just tools for brainstorming; they're actively authoring and editing a significant chunk of the new content we see online. This has huge implications for how we consume information, the quality of content, and the future of online publishing. It means we'll all need to be a bit savvier about what we read.

### Google Helps Publishers Fight Back Against AI Traffic Loss

Speaking of AI-generated content and how we find information, Google is stepping up to help traditional publishers. With AI-driven search potentially sending fewer direct clicks to websites, publishers have been worried about losing traffic. Google is now rolling out a new feature: a button that lets readers designate a publisher as a "preferred source" across Google Search, Discover, and Google News. This could be a lifeline for quality journalism and content creators, helping them retain their audience in an AI-dominated search landscape. If you value a particular news source, this new button will be a simple way to show your support.
[Google gives publishers a new way to fight AI-driven traffic losses](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/20/google-gives-publishers-a-new-way-to-fight-ai-driven-traffic-losses/)

### Meta AI Brings Voice Control to Your Mac Apps

Not to be outdone, Meta AI is also making moves to integrate AI more deeply into our desktop experience. They've launched a new Mac app that essentially lets you talk to all your other applications. Think of it as a universal dictation and command tool. This feature works across all your apps, similar to existing tools like Wispr Flow or Superwhisper, aiming to make your Mac experience more hands-free and efficient. For those who love voice commands or need accessibility features, this could be a game-changer for daily productivity.
[Meta AI’s new Mac app wants you to talk to your apps](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/20/meta-ais-new-mac-app-wants-you-to-talk-to-your-apps/)
