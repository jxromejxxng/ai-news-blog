### OpenAI Faces Scrutiny Over Safety and Accountability

This week, two major stories have put OpenAI's content moderation and safety protocols under a microscope. First, a stalking victim has filed a lawsuit alleging that ChatGPT fueled her abuser's delusions and that OpenAI ignored multiple warnings about the user's dangerous behavior, including its own internal "mass-casualty flag." This is a stark reminder of the very real-world consequences when AI systems are misused or when safety warnings are overlooked. [AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/10/stalking-victim-sues-openai-claims-chatgpt-fueled-her-abusers-delusions-and-ignored-her-warnings/)

Adding to the pressure, the Florida Attorney General has announced an investigation into OpenAI following allegations that ChatGPT was used to plan a shooting at Florida State University last April. The family of one victim plans to sue OpenAI. These incidents highlight a critical challenge for AI developers: how to balance accessibility with robust safety measures, and what level of responsibility they bear when their tools are misused for harmful purposes. For companies building or integrating AI, these cases underscore the absolute necessity of rigorous safety testing, clear usage policies, and responsive moderation systems. ### ChatGPT Introduces a New $100/Month Pro Plan

On a different note, OpenAI has finally rolled out a much-requested $100/month "Pro" plan for ChatGPT. Previously, the pricing jumped from $20/month for Plus to a hefty $200/month for enterprise-level access. This new tier is great news for power users, developers, or small businesses who need more consistent, high-performance access than the standard Plus plan offers, but don't quite need the full enterprise suite. It fills a significant gap in their pricing structure, making advanced ChatGPT capabilities more accessible to a broader segment of dedicated users. If you've been hitting usage limits or needing more reliable access, this could be the sweet spot. [AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/09/chatgpt-pro-plan-100-month-codex/)

### Meta AI App Climbs the Charts After Muse Spark Launch

Meta AI is making waves, with its app soaring to No. 5 on the App Store after the launch of its new Muse Spark model. The app was previously ranked No. 57, so this is a significant jump in popularity. This surge indicates strong user interest and engagement with Meta's latest AI offerings. For anyone tracking the AI landscape, this shows Meta is serious about competing in the consumer AI space. It also means more choices for you, whether you're looking for creative tools, information, or just trying out the latest AI assistant. Keep an eye on how Meta continues to integrate AI across its ecosystem.
