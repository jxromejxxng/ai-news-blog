## Authors Push Back on AI Settlement Splits

The ongoing saga of AI training data and copyright just got another twist. Authors are speaking up, claiming that their publishers and agents are trying to snag an outsized portion of settlement payments from AI companies like Anthropic. This isn't just about money; it's about who owns what, and who deserves to be compensated when creative works are used to train AI models.

**Why this matters for you:**
For creators, this highlights the critical need to understand your contracts and advocate for fair compensation, especially as AI companies increasingly look to leverage existing content. For publishers, agents, and AI companies, it's a reminder that the legal and ethical frameworks around AI are still very much in flux, and transparency and fairness will be key to building long-term trust and avoiding future disputes. Expect more legal battles and contract renegotiations as the industry tries to figure out how to fairly compensate original creators in the age of generative AI.
