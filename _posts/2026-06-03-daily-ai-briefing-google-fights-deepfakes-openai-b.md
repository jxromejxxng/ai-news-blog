## Your Digital Shield Just Got Stronger: Google Fights Deepfake Scams

This is a big one for personal safety. Scammers are getting craftier, not just spoofing numbers but using AI deepfake tech to impersonate people we trust – like family, employers, or authority figures – on calls. It's a scary thought, but Google is rolling out fake call detection to help protect us. This means your phone could soon become a frontline defender against these incredibly sophisticated scams. It's a crucial step in keeping our digital interactions secure and maintaining trust in a world where AI can mimic almost anything.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/02/google-rolls-out-fake-call-detection-to-protect-against-ai-deepfake-impersonation-scams/)

## Level Up Your Workflow: OpenAI's New Tools for White-Collar Pros

For all you knowledge workers out there, OpenAI just dropped a set of six new Codex plug-ins designed to make specific jobs easier. We're talking tools for data analytics, creative production, sales, product design, equity investing, and investment banking. These aren't just generic AI helpers; they bundle integrations, instructions, and context right into the Codex app to approximate the specific tasks of these roles. If you're in one of these fields, this could be a game-changer for automating repetitive tasks, generating insights, or kickstarting creative projects, letting you focus on the higher-level strategy.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/02/openai-launches-new-codex-tools-for-white-collar-work/)

## Even Uber's Feeling the Pinch: AI's Real-World Costs

Here's a dose of reality: even companies as massive as Uber are learning that AI isn't a free lunch. After actively encouraging staff to use AI tools, Uber reportedly blew through its AI budget in just four months and has now capped employee AI spending. This is a clear signal to businesses everywhere: while AI offers incredible potential for efficiency and innovation, it comes with real costs. It highlights the importance of strategic implementation, cost-benefit analysis, and careful budgeting when integrating AI into everyday operations. For employees, it's a reminder that AI tools, while powerful, aren't infinite resources.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/02/uber-caps-employee-ai-spending-after-blowing-through-budget-in-four-months/)

## Scorsese's Secret Weapon? AI for Storyboarding

Who would've thought? Legendary director Martin Scorsese, known for his masterful filmmaking, is now using AI. But before you imagine AI writing his next script, hold on. He's leveraging the tech solely for storyboarding. This is a fantastic example of AI being used as a practical assistant in a creative field, rather than a replacement. It speeds up a labor-intensive part of the filmmaking process, allowing a visionary like Scorsese to visualize scenes more efficiently and focus his human genius on the storytelling itself. It's about augmentation, not automation, for true creative talent.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/02/martin-scorsese-becomes-the-latest-and-most-unlikely-hollywood-voice-for-ai/)

## Behind the Scenes: AI Protecting Our Critical Infrastructure

On a more serious note, Anthropic is significantly scaling its Claude Mythos program, part of Project Glasswing, to protect critical infrastructure across 15 countries. This means AI is being deployed to 150 organizations in sectors like power, water, healthcare, and communications – places where a cyberattack could literally affect hundreds of millions of people. This isn't about productivity apps; it's about using advanced AI models for security vulnerability detection and defense against cyber threats at a national and international level. It's a powerful reminder of AI's crucial, often invisible, role in maintaining the stability of our modern world.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/02/anthropic-scales-claude-mythos-to-critical-infrastructure-in-15-countries/)
