### Hollywood Draws a Line in the Sand: No Oscars for AI-Generated Works

First up, a big one for the creative industry: The Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences (yes, the Oscar folks) has just announced that AI-generated actors and scripts are now officially ineligible for Oscars. This means if a film features a character entirely created by AI, or a screenplay written solely by an AI, it won't be competing for the gold statue.

**Why this matters:** This isn't just about shiny awards; it's a significant move that defines the boundaries of human creativity in a rapidly evolving landscape. For filmmakers, writers, and artists, it clarifies what "human-made" truly means in the age of generative AI. It's a statement about valuing the human touch, emotion, and intent behind storytelling, ensuring that the ultimate credit (and award) goes to the human creators. Bad news for "Tilly Norwood" indeed, but good news for human writers and actors.
