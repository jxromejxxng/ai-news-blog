## Congress Gets Productive with ChatGPT

Turns out, Capitol Hill is all-in on generative AI. House spending records show that OpenAI's ChatGPT is the go-to AI tool for congressional offices. They're using it to draft memos, summarize complex legislation, and even assist with communications to constituents. This is a huge, practical endorsement for AI, showing how it can streamline repetitive tasks and boost productivity in even the most traditional workplaces. If Congress can find daily utility, so can many other organizations!
[Source: TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/03/congresss-favorite-ai-tool-chatgpt/)

## The Human Touch: Teaching AI "Taste"

Ever wonder how AI models get better at understanding what we *really* want? It's often thanks to millions of humans! The creators of Design Arena just raised $7.9 million to expand their work, which involves 5.3 million people globally providing critical human evaluations to frontier AI labs. This "human feedback" is essential for teaching AI models "taste" – making them more aligned with human preferences and values. For anyone building or using AI, this highlights the absolute necessity of integrating human oversight and evaluation to ensure AI tools are not just smart, but also *useful* and *desirable*.
[Source: TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/03/designarena-creators-raise-7-9-million-to-bring-taste-to-ai-models/)

## Simplifying AI for Business: Solving the "Deployment Problem"

One of the biggest hurdles for companies wanting to use AI isn't the AI itself, but how to actually *implement* and *manage* it effectively. Enter June, a new startup backed by Salesforce co-founder Marc Benioff, which just emerged from stealth with $20 million in pre-seed funding. Their mission? To make AI adoption simpler. This is huge for businesses of all sizes. If June can truly streamline the deployment process, it could unlock AI's potential for countless companies that have been intimidated by the complexity. Keep an eye on solutions like this if you're looking to bring AI into your workflow without the headache.
[Source: TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/03/a-marc-benioff-backed-startup-thinks-ai-can-solve-the-ai-deployment-problem/)

## A Word of Caution from Palantir's CEO

While AI adoption is booming, Palantir CEO Alex Karp, fresh off a billion-dollar quarter, issued a strong warning. He called the AI industry "Marxist" and cautioned that many "frontier labs" are too untrustworthy for enterprises. This isn't just philosophical; it's a practical reminder for businesses to carefully vet their AI partners. When choosing AI tools or providers, consider their transparency, security practices, and track record. Trustworthiness, especially with critical business data, remains paramount.
[Source: TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/03/after-killer-quarter-palantir-ceo-alex-karp-calls-ai-industry-marxist/)
