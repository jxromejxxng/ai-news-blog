## AI Makes Web Browsing a Two-Way Street

First up, a really practical update for anyone spending time online (so, basically everyone). **Google is now letting you explore the web side-by-side with its AI Mode.** This means when you're using AI Mode in Chrome on your desktop, clicking a link won't whisk you away from your AI assistant. Instead, it'll open the webpage right next to your AI conversation. Think of it as having a super-smart research assistant always on standby, helping you digest information as you browse. This could be a game-changer for research, learning, or just getting quick summaries without losing your train of thought.
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/16/google-now-lets-you-explore-the-web-side-by-side-with-ai-mode/)

## OpenAI's Codex Gets Even More Powerful on Your Desktop

For the developers and power users out there, **OpenAI's agentic coding tool, Codex, has received a major makeover, giving it significantly more power over your desktop.** This isn't just about writing code anymore; it's about an AI agent that can potentially interact with your desktop environment to perform tasks, automate workflows, and generally be a much more integrated co-pilot. This move puts OpenAI in direct competition with others in the AI agent space, like Anthropic, and signals a future where AI tools aren't just confined to a chat window but can actively help manage your digital workspace. For businesses, this means potentially massive leaps in automation and developer productivity.
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/16/openai-takes-aim-at-anthropic-with-beefed-up-codex-that-gives-it-more-power-over-your-desktop/)

## Robots Are Learning Tasks They Weren't Taught

This next one is a peek into the future of physical AI. **Physical Intelligence, a hot robotics startup, announced its new robot brain, called π0.7, can figure out tasks it was never explicitly taught.** This is a really big deal because it’s an early but meaningful step toward what we've all been dreaming of: a general-purpose robot brain. Imagine a robot that can adapt to new environments and unexpected situations without needing a human to reprogram it for every new scenario. For industries like manufacturing, logistics, and even home assistance, this kind of adaptive intelligence could unlock unprecedented levels of automation and flexibility. We're still early, but the groundwork for truly intelligent, versatile robots is being laid.
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/16/physical-intelligence-a-hot-robotics-startup-says-its-new-robot-brain-can-figure-out-tasks-it-was-never-taught/)

## AI is Boosting Retail Revenue (and Traffic!)

Here's some tangible proof of AI's impact on the bottom line: **AI traffic to US retailers surged by an incredible 393% in Q1, and it's directly boosting their revenue.** Adobe's data shows that visitors coming through AI-powered channels (think AI search, personalized recommendations, AI chatbots, etc.) are not only visiting more but also converting better and generating more revenue than traditional shoppers. This isn't just a trend; it's a clear signal that consumers are embracing AI-driven shopping experiences, and businesses that leverage AI effectively are seeing real financial gains. If you're in e-commerce, ignoring AI is no longer an option.
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/16/ai-traffic-to-us-retailers-rose-393-in-Q1-and-its-boosting-their-revenue-too/)

## Hollywood's Big Picture Shift: 50 Films for the Price of One

Finally, let's talk about creativity. **Runway's CEO suggests AI could help Hollywood produce 50 films for the cost of one $100 million blockbuster.** This isn't just about saving money; it's about democratizing filmmaking and allowing for a massive increase in creative output. Imagine the sheer volume of diverse stories and experimental projects that could see the light of day if production costs plummeted. This could completely change the landscape of entertainment, leading to more niche content, faster production cycles, and potentially more hits by simply increasing the number of attempts. It's an exciting prospect for creators and audiences alike.
