## ChatGPT Goes Unlimited (and Gets Smarter!) for Free Users

This is a big one for pretty much everyone. OpenAI is now giving **free ChatGPT users unlimited text chats!** No more hitting a cap and waiting. This means you can use it for brainstorming, drafting emails, coding help, or just satisfying your curiosity without constantly checking your usage.

But wait, there's more! Free and 'Go' users are also getting a new **"think button"** for complex queries. Think of this as a little prompt for ChatGPT to take a moment, process your request more deeply, and come back with a more thoughtful, nuanced answer. This is fantastic for tackling tricky problems where you need more than a surface-level response. It's a clear move to make AI more accessible and useful for daily tasks without costing a dime.
[Source: AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/06/openai-brings-unlimited-chatgpt-text-chats-to-free-users/)

## AI Takes on Business Grunt Work: Automating Your Startup (Seriously)

Starting a company often means drowning in paperwork and administrative tasks. But a new startup called **Naïve** just raised a hefty $28.5 million to tackle exactly that problem. They claim their "infra" can automate most of the tedious, manual work involved in setting up and running a business.

Imagine if AI could handle the initial legal filings, setting up your basic financial systems, or even managing early operational logistics. This isn't just about making things a little easier; it's about fundamentally reducing the time, cost, and headache of getting a venture off the ground. For aspiring entrepreneurs or small business owners, this could be a game-changer, freeing you up to focus on your core product or service.
[Source: AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/06/naive-raises-28-5m-to-automate-the-grunt-work-of-setting-up-and-running-a-company/)

## Smarter Shopping, Thanks to Ex-Spotify AI

Ever wonder how Spotify always seems to know exactly what song you want to hear next? That incredibly powerful recommendation engine is now making its way to e-commerce! A team of ex-Spotify employees has raised $10 million to bring that same intelligent AI to online retail.

Their platform is designed to predict which product a shopper wants next, learn their general taste over time, and continuously fine-tune its recommendations based on what they do in real-time. For online businesses, this means potentially higher conversion rates and a much more personalized shopping experience for customers. For us shoppers, it means less sifting through irrelevant items and more quickly finding things we'll genuinely love. It's like having a personal shopper who knows your style before you even do.
[Source: AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/06/ex-spotify-employees-raise-10m-to-bring-the-ai-behind-its-recommendations-to-e-commerce/)

## Customer Support Gets a Brain Boost with Omilia's AI

Nobody likes waiting on hold or navigating frustrating automated phone menus. Good news: AI is making customer support much more efficient and user-friendly. **Omilia** just raised $67 million to scale its AI-powered customer support platform, having already grown its annual recurring revenue tenfold since 2020.

This kind of AI isn't just about chatbots; it's about intelligent systems that can understand complex customer queries, provide accurate solutions quickly, and even anticipate needs. For businesses, this means reducing operational costs and improving customer satisfaction. For us, the consumers, it means getting help faster, with less hassle, and potentially even having more complex issues resolved by AI without needing to speak to a human (though a human is always there for escalation, of course!).
