## OpenAI Merges ChatGPT Usage, Expect New Limits

If you're a heavy ChatGPT user, especially for work, listen up. OpenAI is rolling out a new system that combines your "Chat" and "Work" usage into a single meter. This means if you're bouncing between asking ChatGPT for creative ideas and using it to draft emails or code, you might hit your usage limits sooner than before. It's a good reminder to be mindful of your prompts and how much you lean on the tool throughout the day. For businesses, this might mean re-evaluating how teams integrate ChatGPT into their daily workflows to avoid hitting caps.

## New York Bans AI Chatbot Toys for Kids

Parents, if you were thinking of getting an AI chatbot toy for your kid this Christmas, you might want to hold off, especially if you're in New York. The state has enacted a five-year ban on these toys, citing new research that highlights troubling safety failures. The concerns include exposure to inappropriate content and potential risks to a child's emotional development. This is a significant move that underscores the growing scrutiny around AI products designed for children and the need for robust safety standards. It's a clear signal for both consumers to be cautious and for toy manufacturers to prioritize safety and ethical design.
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/want-to-buy-your-kid-an-ai-toy-this-Christmas-not-in-New-York/)

## State AGs Challenge Character.AI's Plan to Auction Private Chats

Here's a major privacy alert for anyone using AI chatbots. Character.AI is reportedly planning a bankruptcy sale that includes sensitive personal data from over 21 million U.S. accounts – and a concerning 2.1 million of those belong to minors. State Attorneys General are pushing back hard against this, and for good reason. This situation is a stark reminder of the importance of understanding a service's privacy policy and being extremely cautious about what personal information you share with any AI chatbot. Your private conversations might not stay private forever, especially if a company faces financial troubles.
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/state-ags-fight-character-ais-plan-to-auction-off-users-private-chats/)

## Building AI Data Centers is Getting Tougher

On the infrastructure side of things, it's becoming increasingly difficult and expensive to build new AI data centers. Nvidia, a key player in AI hardware, is making it clear they won't backstop a data center project until it's almost ready to go. This, combined with construction delays and higher debt costs, is creating a bottleneck for "neoclouds" – the newer, specialized cloud providers focusing on AI. For developers and businesses, this could mean higher costs for AI compute power down the line, or slower expansion of advanced AI services. It highlights that the physical world of land, power, and construction is still a massive factor in the digital AI revolution.
