## Perplexity's Personal Computer for Mac is Here

This is a big one for Mac users! Perplexity, known for its conversational search engine, has made its "Personal Computer" AI agent available to everyone on Mac. Imagine having an AI assistant that lives directly on your computer, ready to help you with tasks, research, and organization in a deeply integrated way. This isn't just a chatbot in a browser; it's an AI agent designed to work *with* your operating system, potentially changing how we interact with our devices and get things done. It's a step towards truly personalized, always-on AI assistance.

[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/07/perplexitys-personal-computer-is-now-available-everyone-on-mac/)

## OpenAI Enhances User Safety with 'Trusted Contact' Feature

In a crucial move for user well-being, OpenAI is introducing a new 'Trusted Contact' safeguard. This feature is designed to provide an extra layer of protection for ChatGPT users, particularly in sensitive situations where conversations might indicate possible self-harm. It's a responsible step by OpenAI to address the ethical challenges of powerful AI and ensure there are mechanisms in place to help users in distress. This shows a commitment to not just developing advanced AI, but also ensuring its use is as safe as possible for everyone.

[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/07/openai-introduces-new-trusted-contact-safeguard-for-cases-of-possible-self-harm/)

## Spotify Aims to Be Your Hub for AI-Generated Audio

Spotify is making a strong play to become the go-to platform for AI-generated personal audio. This means you could soon be able to create a podcast using AI tools like Codex or Claude Code and directly import it into Spotify. Think about the implications for independent creators, marketers, or even just hobbyists who want to quickly generate audio content without needing a full production setup. This could democratize audio content creation, making it easier for anyone to bring their ideas to life in sound.

[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/07/spotify-wants-to-become-the-home-for-ai-generated-personal-audio/)
