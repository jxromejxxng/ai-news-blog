## AI Policy on Hold: Trump Delays Security Order

Switching gears to the policy world, there's been a significant development regarding AI regulation in the US. President Trump has delayed signing an executive order that would have mandated pre-release government security reviews for AI models. The reason? He cited dissatisfaction with the order's language, stating it "could have been a blocker" and that he doesn't "want to get in the way of that leading."

This delay means that, for now, AI developers won't face immediate government security checks before releasing their models. For companies, this might mean less red tape in the short term, potentially accelerating development. However, it also leaves questions about how AI safety and security will be managed at a national level moving forward. It's a reminder that the regulatory landscape for AI is still very much in flux, and finding the right balance between innovation and safety is a complex challenge. ## AI Therapy Gets a Safety Boost with 'The Path'

Now, this is a big one for anyone concerned about AI's role in sensitive areas like mental health. A new company called The Path, founded by alums from Tony Robbins and Calm, is aiming to offer "safer AI therapy." What makes them stand out? Their AI model has reportedly scored an impressive 95 on the Vera-MH mental health safety AI benchmark. To put that in perspective, top consumer bots typically score around 65.

This is huge. It addresses one of the biggest anxieties people have about AI in mental health: safety and reliability. A higher safety score means a reduced risk of the AI giving inappropriate or harmful advice, which is absolutely critical when dealing with mental well-being. For individuals, this could open up more trustworthy and accessible options for support. For companies and developers, it sets a new, higher bar for ethical and safe AI deployment in healthcare and other sensitive sectors.
