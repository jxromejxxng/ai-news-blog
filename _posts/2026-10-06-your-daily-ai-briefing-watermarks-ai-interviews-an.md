## What's New in AI Today?

### 1. OpenAI to Watermark ChatGPT Content in the EU

First up, a big step for transparency and trust in AI. OpenAI is planning to start watermarking text generated by ChatGPT and Codex specifically for users in the EU. This move is to comply with the upcoming EU AI Act, which aims to set clear rules for AI development and deployment.

**Why this matters to you:**
For anyone creating content, whether it's marketing copy, reports, or creative writing, this means there'll be an invisible mark indicating it came from an AI. While OpenAI admits editing might make these marks harder to detect, it's a significant push towards helping us distinguish between human and AI-generated text. This could be a game-changer for academic integrity, journalistic ethics, and simply knowing what you're reading.

[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/openai-will-start-watermarking-chatgpts-text-in-the-eu/)

### 2. AI Takes the Interview Hot Seat with HackerRank

Job seekers, listen up! HackerRank's AI interviewer is already making waves, having conducted over half a million interviews. Major companies like Snowflake, Snorkel, and Capgemini are among the early adopters.

**Why this matters to you:**
If you're looking for a job, you might soon find your first conversation isn't with a human recruiter, but an AI. This could mean more consistent, unbiased initial screenings, but it also means adapting your interview prep to an AI that's programmed to assess specific skills and responses. For companies, it's a way to scale hiring, reduce bias, and potentially find talent faster. It's a glimpse into the future of recruitment, and it's here now.

[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/hackerranks-ai-interviewer-offers-a-glimpse-into-what-job-interviews-could-become/)

### 3. TikTok's AI Shopping Assistant Wants to Be Your Personal Shopper

Get ready for a smarter shopping experience on TikTok. The social media giant is rolling out an AI shopping assistant and a one-click checkout feature. This conversational AI agent is designed to help you discover and purchase products directly within the app.

**Why this matters to you:**
If you're a TikTok user, this means less scrolling and more direct interaction to find what you're looking for. Imagine asking an AI for recommendations based on your style or needs, and then buying it with a single tap. For businesses, this is a massive opportunity to connect with customers through an intelligent, personalized shopping journey right where they're already spending their time.

[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/tiktok-rolls-out-an-ai-shopping-assistant-and-one-click-checkout/)

### 4. Instinct Brings AI Agents to Your Group Chats

Planning with friends just got a whole lot easier. Instinct is launching group chats that allow friends to use its AI agent together for tasks like organizing carpools, planning trips, and coordinating events. The cool part? Even friends without an Instinct account can join in.

**Why this matters to you:**
No more endless back-and-forth messages trying to nail down logistics. This AI agent can act as your personal group assistant, handling the nitty-gritty details of planning. While personal accounts remain separate and require permission before sharing info, it’s a practical step towards making AI a seamless part of our social coordination, saving you time and headaches.

[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/instinct-brings-its-ai-agent-to-group-chats-even-for-friends-without-an-account/)
