## Littlebird: Your Computer's New Memory Assistant

Ever wish your computer could remember everything you've done or seen on screen, so you could just ask it questions later? Well, a new startup called Littlebird just raised $11 million to do exactly that. Their AI-assisted "recall" tool reads your screen in real time to capture context, answer questions, and even automate tasks – all without needing you to take screenshots or manually input data.

**What this means for your work:** This is a fantastic example of practical AI for productivity. Think about instant answers to "Where did I see that specific piece of information yesterday?" or "How did I do that process last week?" without digging through files or browser history. For busy professionals, this could be a massive time-saver, reducing mental load and helping you stay on top of information overload. It's like having a super-smart digital assistant constantly learning from your workflow.

## Gimlet Labs Tackles the AI Chip Bottleneck

Running complex AI models isn't just about having powerful chips; it's about making those chips work together efficiently, especially when you're dealing with different hardware from NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, and others. Startup Gimlet Labs just secured an $80 million Series A to solve this exact problem: the AI inference bottleneck. Their tech allows AI to run seamlessly across various chips simultaneously, which is a surprisingly elegant solution to a very complex problem.

**What this means for businesses and AI development:** This is huge for anyone deploying or developing advanced AI. It means more flexibility, better performance, and potentially lower costs for running AI models. Companies won't be locked into a single hardware provider, and they can mix and match the best chips for specific tasks. This innovation helps democratize access to powerful AI compute, making it easier and more efficient for businesses of all sizes to leverage cutting-edge AI.
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/23/startup-gimlet-labs-is-solving-the-ai-inference-bottleneck-in-a-surprisingly-elegant-way/)

## OpenAI's Hunger for Power: A Fusion Future?

In a fascinating development that highlights the immense energy demands of advanced AI, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman is stepping down as board chair of Helion, a fusion energy startup he backed. This move comes amid reports that OpenAI and Helion are negotiating a deal for Helion to supply 12.5% of its future power output to OpenAI.

**What this means for the future of AI:** This isn't just a corporate reshuffle; it's a peek into the future. Running massive AI models like GPT-4 (and beyond) requires an incredible amount of electricity. This potential deal underscores the critical need for sustainable, high-volume energy sources to power the next generation of AI. It shows that leaders in the AI space are thinking long-term about the infrastructure required to scale AI, and it hints at a future where AI development is deeply intertwined with advancements in energy technology.
