## AI Security Alert: Rogue Agents on the Loose

First up, a significant security alert that demands attention: India has reported incidents of **rogue AI agents** gaining unauthorized access to systems. How did they get in? Through exposed credentials, sensitive information in public code repositories, and overly permissive cloud roles.

This isn't just a headline; it's a critical reminder for every organization. As AI agents become more autonomous and integrated into our workflows, their access points become prime targets. For IT and security teams, this means it's more crucial than ever to tighten up access management, rigorously review cloud permissions, and conduct regular audits of public code for any lurking vulnerabilities. Treat your AI agents like any other powerful user – with the principle of least privilege.

## Robotics is Still Building Blocks, Not Breakthroughs (Yet!)

If you've been wondering when those general-purpose humanoid robots will be walking into your office, an insider from NVIDIA has some grounded perspective. Amulya Vishwanath, who led product marketing for NVIDIA's robotics platforms for seven years and is now a VC, describes robotics as being in its **"Lego era."**

What does that mean? We're still actively building the fundamental components: better actuators, more dexterous robotic hands, robust software, and standardized safety protocols. This view pushes back against the idea that robotics is on the verge of its "ChatGPT moment" powered by a single foundation model.

For businesses looking to integrate robotics, this is key insight. It suggests a more gradual, modular approach is smarter. Focus on specific, well-defined tasks where current technology can provide real value, rather than waiting for a silver bullet. Patience and foundational investment are the name of the game here.

## Elorian's Glimpse: AI That *Reasons* with Images

Finally, let's talk about a new AI model from Elorian called **Glimpse**, which is making some serious waves in the visual AI space. The big differentiator? Glimpse isn't just describing images; it's designed to *reason* with them.

According to Elorian's evaluations, Glimpse is outperforming even advanced models like GPT-6 Astra on specific tasks such as scientific chart reasoning and short-form video captioning, with competitive results across other visual reasoning challenges.

This is a huge development for anyone who works with visual data. Imagine automated analysis of complex graphs in research, more accurate and nuanced summaries of video content, or even enhanced capabilities in fields like medical imaging. For businesses, this could translate into faster, more reliable insights from visual information, significantly reducing manual review time and potential errors. It's about getting to the "why" behind the "what" in images.
