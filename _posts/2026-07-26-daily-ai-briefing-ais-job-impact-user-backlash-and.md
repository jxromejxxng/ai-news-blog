## AI's Shifting Job Landscape: It's Not Just About Robots

First up, let's talk about jobs. Monday.com is the latest big name to announce layoffs, explicitly citing AI as a factor. They're not alone; TechCrunch has been tracking a growing list of over 20 major tech companies this year that have pointed to AI as a reason for workforce reductions.

**What this means for you:** This isn't just a dystopian future where robots take over. It's about companies re-evaluating roles and workflows. When AI tools can handle certain tasks more efficiently, it changes the demand for human skills. For individuals, this highlights the importance of adapting, learning AI-assisted tools, and focusing on uniquely human skills like critical thinking, creativity, and complex problem-solving. For companies, it means a strategic re-think of team structures and talent development. It's a challenging period, but also one that pushes us to evolve.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/25/the-running-list-major-tech-layoffs-in-2026-where-employers-cited-ai/)

## The "Avoiding AI" Movement Gains Traction

On the flip side of embracing AI, we're seeing a fascinating counter-movement emerge. Librarians across the country are hosting "Avoiding AI" workshops, and get this – they're seeing unprecedented demand! People are actively seeking ways to opt-out, minimize, or simply understand how to navigate a world increasingly permeated by AI, especially if they're feeling fed up with Big Tech's influence.

**What this means for you:** This isn't just a niche trend; it's a clear signal that there's a growing desire for digital boundaries and personal agency in the age of AI. For individuals, these workshops offer practical strategies to regain some control over their digital lives. For companies and AI developers, it's a crucial reminder that not everyone wants AI integrated into every aspect of their existence. User sentiment matters, and understanding this pushback is key for responsible innovation and building trust.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/25/librarians-are-hosting-viral-avoiding-ai-workshops-for-people-who-are-fed-up-with-big-tech/)

## Powering AI: The Hidden Infrastructure Challenge

Finally, let's talk about something less glamorous but absolutely critical: power. A recent incident in Northern Virginia, where a single fallen power line exposed significant vulnerabilities in how AI data centers respond to grid disruptions, is a wake-up call.

**What this means for you and businesses:** AI models require immense computing power, which translates to massive data centers consuming staggering amounts of electricity. This isn't just an environmental concern; it's a fundamental operational one. If our power grids aren't robust and resilient enough to handle the ever-increasing demand from AI, we're looking at potential reliability issues and outages. For any business relying on cloud-based AI services, or even just general digital infrastructure, this highlights a critical vulnerability. We need smarter, more resilient energy solutions to truly sustain the AI revolution.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/25/one-fallen-power-line-exposed-a-growing-ai-data-center-problem-heres-how-to-fix-it/)

## Wrapping Up

It's prompting us to rethink not just our jobs, but our relationship with technology itself, and even the very infrastructure that powers it all. Staying informed and adaptable is key, whether you're embracing AI, trying to avoid it, or just trying to keep the lights on. That's it for today's briefing – catch you next time!
