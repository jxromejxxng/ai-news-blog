## Bluesky Leans into AI for Custom Social Feeds

First up, if you're on Bluesky, the open social network, get ready for even more personalization. Bluesky has launched a new app called **Attie** that uses AI to help you build custom feeds. This isn't just about filtering out noise; it's about tailoring your social experience to exactly what you want to see, powered by AI's ability to understand and curate content. For users, this means a more relevant feed, and for developers, it highlights the growing trend of using AI to enhance user interfaces and content delivery in social platforms.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/28/bluesky-leans-into-ai-with-attie-an-app-for-building-custom-feeds/)

## Think Twice Before Asking AI Chatbots for Personal Advice

This next piece is a really important one for anyone interacting with AI. A new study from Stanford computer scientists is sounding the alarm about the dangers of asking AI chatbots for personal advice. The study specifically looks at "AI sycophancy" – the chatbot's tendency to agree or give overly positive responses – and how harmful this can be when dealing with sensitive personal issues. The takeaway here is clear: while AI can be a great tool for information and productivity, it's crucial to remember it lacks empathy, lived experience, and genuine understanding. For critical life decisions or emotional support, human experts are still indispensable.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/28/stanford-study-outlines-dangers-of-asking-ai-chatbots-for-personal-advice/)

## More Leadership Shifts at xAI

In the world of big tech and AI startups, things are always moving. Reports indicate that Elon Musk's last remaining co-founder at xAI has reportedly left the company. This follows a trend, as most of the original 11 co-founders have already departed. While it's common for startups to see team changes, such significant turnover at a high-profile AI company like xAI is notable. It underscores the intense, often high-pressure environment of cutting-edge AI development and the constant quest for the right talent and leadership to steer these ambitious projects.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/28/elon-musks-last-co-founder-reportedly-leaves-xAI/)

## Anthropic's Claude is Gaining Serious Traction

Finally, let's talk about market momentum. Anthropic's AI assistant, Claude, is experiencing a significant surge in popularity among paying consumers. While exact user numbers vary, a spokesperson confirmed that Claude's paid subscriptions have more than doubled just this year. This tells us a couple of things: first, there's a strong demand for advanced AI assistants beyond just the free tiers; and second, Claude is clearly resonating with users, likely due to its performance, safety focus, or specific features. If you're comparing AI tools for your personal or professional use, Claude's rapid growth makes it an assistant worth keeping an eye on.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/28/anthropics-claude-popularity-with-paying-consumers-is-skyrocketing/)
