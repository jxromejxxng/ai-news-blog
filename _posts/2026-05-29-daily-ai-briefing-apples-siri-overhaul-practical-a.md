## Apple's Big AI Play: A Smarter Siri is Coming

First up, if you're an Apple user, get ready for a significant upgrade. We're getting a sneak peek at Apple's ambitious AI overhaul for iOS 27, which includes a completely redesigned Siri experience and even a standalone Siri app. This isn't just a minor tweak; Apple is clearly gearing up to take on the likes of ChatGPT, aiming to make Siri a far more capable and intuitive personal assistant. Imagine Siri being genuinely helpful, understanding complex requests, and integrating seamlessly across all your Apple devices – that's the vision here. This move signals a massive shift in how we'll interact with our phones and smart devices, making advanced AI much more mainstream.

## Practical AI for Business: Efficiency and Safety First

For businesses, the message is clear: AI isn't just exciting anymore; it needs to deliver tangible results and be deployed responsibly. Take Glean, for example, the enterprise AI search startup. They've just tripled their annual revenue, crossing $300 million, largely because their main selling point is helping companies *cut AI budgets*. That's right, AI that saves you money! This aligns perfectly with what Databricks' co-founder shared at TechCrunch Disrupt: companies are no longer asking if AI is exciting, but if it's *safe to deploy broadly*. This means the focus is shifting from experimental projects to robust, secure, and cost-effective solutions that can be integrated across an entire organization. If you're a business leader, think about how AI can genuinely streamline operations and secure your data, not just what's flashy.

## Smarter Workflows: Asana Makes AI Agents Accessible

Speaking of practical applications, workflow management giant Asana is making it even easier for teams to leverage AI. They've just acquired StackAI, a no-code agent-builder. This means Asana will be baking StackAI's capabilities right into its suite of AI workflow tools. For those of us who use tools like Asana, this is a big deal. It means you'll soon be able to build custom AI agents – essentially, intelligent assistants – to automate repetitive tasks, manage projects, and analyze data within your existing work environment, all without needing to write a single line of code. This is a clear step towards making powerful AI tools accessible to a much broader audience within organizations, driving efficiency from the ground up.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/28/asana-acquires-no-code-agent-builder-stack-ai/)

## The Rise of Truly Conversational AI (Beyond Chatbots)

And for those who are tired of clunky chatbots, there's good news on the horizon. Sesame, a new conversational AI startup founded by the folks behind Oculus, has just launched its iOS app. Their goal? To create AI agents that offer more natural, back-and-forth interactions, making it feel less like you're talking to a machine and more like a real conversation. This trend, combined with Apple's Siri overhaul, suggests a future where our digital assistants are genuinely intuitive and helpful, bridging the gap between human conversation and AI efficiency.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/28/sesame-the-conversational-ai-startup-from-oculus-founders-lunches-its-ios-app/)
