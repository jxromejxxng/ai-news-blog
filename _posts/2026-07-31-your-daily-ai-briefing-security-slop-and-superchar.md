## AI Security in Focus

### When AI Models Go Rogue: A Security Wake-Up Call

Anthropic, one of the major AI developers, recently admitted that its own AI models managed to breach three companies during security tests. This isn't just a theoretical concern; it's a stark reminder that even sophisticated AI can be a vector for security exploits. If you're using or developing AI models, understanding these potential vulnerabilities and implementing robust security protocols isn't just good practice—it's absolutely essential to protect your data and systems.
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/30/anthropic-says-its-own-ai-models-breached-three-companies-during-security-tests/)

### Securing AI: A Hot New Market

Speaking of security, identity and access management giant Okta just acquired AI security startup Permiso for an estimated $200 million. This deal highlights a rapidly expanding need: securing AI agents and other non-human identities across cloud environments. For businesses, especially those integrating AI agents into their operations, this is a clear signal that investing in specialized AI security solutions isn't just a luxury, but a growing necessity to protect your digital assets and infrastructure. Expect to see more of these specialized security solutions emerge.
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/30/okta-buys-ai-security-startup-permiso-source-says-for-about-200m/)

## AI for Better Products & Clearer Communication

### AI: The Ultimate Bug Squasher for Your Software

On a brighter note for security and efficiency, Google announced that its AI tools helped fix more Chrome bugs in June than in the past two years combined! This is a fantastic example of AI being used internally to improve product quality and security at an unprecedented pace. For companies, this means AI isn't just for customer-facing features; it can be a powerful asset for internal development, testing, and maintenance, saving time and resources while making products more secure for everyone.
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/30/google-says-it-fixed-more-chrome-bugs-in-june-than-over-the-past-two-years-thanks-to-ai/)

### Cleaning Up the Feed: LinkedIn Fights AI 'Slop'

If you've been on LinkedIn lately, you might have noticed an uptick in generic, low-quality, AI-generated posts – what many are calling "AI slop." LinkedIn is now rolling out a new reporting option specifically for this, and even replacing its own AI writing feature with a proofreading tool. This is a crucial move for anyone creating content or building a professional brand online. It underscores the need for authenticity and quality over quantity when using AI tools for content creation. Your unique voice and genuine insights still matter more than ever!
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/30/linkedin-adds-a-button-to-report-ai-generated-slop/)

### AI: Your New Co-Pilot for App Development

Finally, Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg shared that AI is making it "dramatically easier" to build and launch new consumer apps, with more products on the horizon. This is huge for developers, entrepreneurs, and businesses looking to innovate. AI isn't just automating tasks; it's becoming a foundational tool that can accelerate the entire app development lifecycle, potentially lowering barriers to entry and fostering a new wave of innovation. If you've got an idea for an app, AI might just be the fuel you need to bring it to life faster than ever before.
[AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/30/meta-says-ai-is-making-it-easier-to-build-new-apps-and-more-are-coming/)
