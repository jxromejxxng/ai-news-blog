### The Reality of AI's Financial Payoff for Businesses

Now, for something that might hit closer to home for many companies. An economist from Apollo is making waves by stating that despite all the massive investments and hype, AI has delivered "zero financial payoff" to most companies outside of the tech sector. This is a crucial reality check. It's not that AI isn't powerful, but simply throwing money at AI tools or projects without a clear, strategic plan for integration and problem-solving isn't yielding returns. For businesses, this means focusing less on the *idea* of AI and more on specific use cases that genuinely improve efficiency, reduce costs, or open new revenue streams. Don't just invest in AI; invest in *how* you'll use it to create tangible value.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/25/ai-delivers-zero-financial-payoff-to-most-companies-according-to-apollo-economist/)

### Hot New AI Wearables and Gadgets

On a more personal tech note, AI wearables are heating up! TechCrunch has compiled a list of some of the "hottest" new gadgets hitting the market, including names like Muse Charm, Bee, Friend, and Plaud. These aren't just incremental updates; they're devices designed to integrate AI directly into your daily life, often focusing on subtle, always-on assistance. Think smarter note-taking, real-time language translation, contextual information access, or advanced health monitoring, all in a more seamless, less intrusive form factor than a phone. These gadgets aim to make AI an even more invisible, yet indispensable, part of your routine. It's an exciting space to watch for practical applications of AI.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/25/the-hottest-ai-wearables-and-gadgets-you-should-know-about/)
